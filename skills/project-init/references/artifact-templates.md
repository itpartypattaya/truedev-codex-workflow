# Artifact templates

Use these only when the repository has no established format. Keep entries concise and link to the
source evidence or decision record where appropriate.

## REQUIREMENTS.md

```markdown
# Requirements

## Source and revision
## Problem and measurable outcome
## Target users and stakeholders
## Functional requirements
- FR-001: ...
## Non-functional requirements
- NFR-001: ...
## Constraints
## Explicit exclusions
## Open questions
## Assumptions requiring approval
```

## prd.md

```markdown
# Product Requirements Document

## Vision and MVP boundary
## Users and needs
## Prioritized stories and acceptance criteria
## Key flows and recovery paths
## Success metrics
## Legal, privacy, and policy requirements
## Risks and mitigations
## Out of scope
## Decisions and sign-offs
```

## architecture.md

```markdown
# Architecture and Technical Design

## Context and constraints
## System and component boundaries
## Data model and contracts
## Identity, authorization, and security
## Failure handling and observability
## Deployment, migrations, backup, and rollback
## Verification strategy
## Robustness coverage and evidence
## Risks, deferred decisions, and owner sign-offs
## Source versions and verification dates
```
